How it works

Two ways to book

Pay-as-you-go

You pay a hiring fee, and your guests buy their own treats on the day.

All-inclusive

You pay a hiring fee plus a set number of items per guest upfront. Your guests just enjoy.

For both options: please provide electricity at the venue, with a plug point close to where we set up.
