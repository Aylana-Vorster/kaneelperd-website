How it works

Two ways to book

Pay-as-you-go

You pay a hiring fee, and your guests buy their own treats on the day. Great for big staff parties.

All-inclusive

You pay a hiring fee plus a set number of treats per guest upfront. Your team just enjoys.

Good to know: minimum 2 hours, a 50% deposit secures your date, and we need electricity with a plug point close to where we set up.

Questions

Year-end function FAQs

How far in advance should we book a year-end function?

As soon as you have a date. November and December fill up quickly, and a 50% deposit secures your date.

Do you travel outside Centurion?

Yes. Travel within Centurion is free. For venues further away, such as Pretoria or Midrand, we add a small travel fee to the quote.

What do you need from the venue?

Electricity, with a plug point close to where we set up, and enough space for the stations. Tell us if a car with a trailer can get close.

Can our guests pay for their own treats?

Yes. With pay-as-you-go you pay a hiring fee and guests buy their own treats on the day. With all-inclusive you pay upfront for a set number of treats per guest.

How long can you stay?

Bookings are for a minimum of 2 hours. Longer functions are no problem, just tell us your start time and how long you need us.

Do you cater proper meals too?

Yes. Chef Danté also caters meals for events. Ask for a catering quote with your date, number of guests and what you have in mind.
